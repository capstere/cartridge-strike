# FPS props in the design studio

The FPS catalogue adds 16 placeable objects under the **FPS & labbutrustning** library filter. The same render adapters are used by the furniture studio, level studio and saved-project FPS preview. Catalogue data stays independent of Three.js; geometry is built only when an object is placed or previewed.

### BINDER vacuum oven

No separately named BINDER/vacuum-oven model was found in those source trees. The new model is labelled as a reconstruction in `fps-catalog.mjs`. It has a white cabinet, red controller, square transparent door, locking handle, steel chamber and three shelves. It is distinct from both the existing white/red PQC instrument and the recovered drying-oven GLB.

Visual reference: [BINDER VD 56 manufacturer page](https://www.binder-world.com/us-en/products/drying-ovens/vacuum-drying-chambers/product/vd-56). Geometry and the small controller label are generated by this project; no manufacturer CAD file or product photograph is redistributed. Its catalogue footprint is approximately 0.638 m wide, 0.55 m deep including the handle, and 0.815 m high. These are game-placement dimensions.

## Rendering and placement

Every procedural object is centred on X/Z, rebased to Y=0, and fitted to its saved width/depth/height. Small laboratory props are countertop objects; carts, benches, cabinets and the open drying oven are floor objects. The grille and strip light use wall mounting. The cart exposes its two real shelf tops at 0.190 m and 0.835 m after rebasing, allowing mounted props to follow the cart when moved. The laboratory bench exposes the free right-hand worktop at 0.9125 m; its pre-existing central instrument and tubes are outside that usable area.

The Cartridge retains the game's screen-size LOD, including parent-transform-aware sample bounds and hysteresis. The six-cartridge tray uses six sample bounds and a single merged cluster per material/detail level. Its distant cartridge geometry totals 2,946 triangles, plus the tray. Only one detail level is visible at a time.

Templates share immutable geometry and materials across placed instances; each placement owns an independent object hierarchy and selection IDs. They are not marked as disposable per-instance geometry/materials. No gameplay entities, physics callbacks, moving parts or extra lights are created. The current Cartridge factory's source notices and metadata are unchanged.

## Verification

Run the focused tests from the repository root:

```sh
node --experimental-loader ./tools/vendor-three-test-loader.mjs --test furniture-editor/library/fps-furniture.test.mjs
```

They check resized and grounded bounds, finite geometry, instance independence, shared resource ownership, exact Cartridge geometry reuse, all LOD transitions after parent translation, BINDER's transparent chamber, physical cart/worktop heights, and local GLB integrity/attribution.

`tools/studio-lab-browser.mjs` renders every shipped object using the common project renderer. Its `GENERATE_THUMBS=1 THUMBS_ONLY=1` mode produces the catalogue thumbnails from those actual meshes.
