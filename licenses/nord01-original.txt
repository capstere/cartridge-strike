NORD-01 selected facility models — original project assets

NORD-01 Facility FPS Essentials was authored as original procedural geometry in
capstere/onboard. Its graphite/teal/ivory materials use authored constants rather
than external textures. No third-party meshes, company logos, production data,
photographs, proprietary reference meshes or commercial-game assets were used.

The Cartridge Strike facility sequence uses six model types: access terminal,
wall vent, strip light, security locker, supply crate and medical case. Nine GLB
runtime derivatives are generated by tools/assets/export_facility_assets.py
from asset-portfolio/nord-01/source/build_assets.py. The exporter applies the
authored bevel modifiers, which the portfolio's previous GLB export omitted.
The original portfolio GLBs and editable master are not included in runtime.

These are project-owned assets, not a new third-party asset license or a grant
of rights in private source documents. Public distribution of these selected
runtime derivatives requires the owner's explicit approval recorded in the
byte-bound release policy. The owner explicitly approved public distribution of the nine listed in-game runtime derivatives on 2026-10-25 for this release only. The rest of the private portfolio, its source files and private original documents remain excluded.
