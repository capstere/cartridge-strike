# Cartridge Strike: actual third-party reuse

Audited 2026-10-14. Runtime dependencies and assets are local. Exact files, source archive hashes and individual SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [docs/fps-assets.json](docs/fps-assets.json). `package-lock.json` pins npm package integrity. Private floor plans and photographs were not sent to any asset provider.

## Three.js 0.180.0 — MIT

Copyright © 2010–2025 Three.js authors. Full upstream license: [licenses/three-LICENSE](licenses/three-LICENSE).

Source: [Three.js r180](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r180). Vendored in `vendor/three/`: `three.core.js`, `three.module.js`, `addons/math/Capsule.js`, `addons/math/Octree.js`, `addons/loaders/GLTFLoader.js`, `addons/utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js`, and `addons/geometries/RoundedBoxGeometry.js`.

Three supplies rendering, GLB asset loading and actual Capsule/Octree collision, using the project's accepted floor geometry. Collision/movement follows the approach inspected in the [official r180 FPS example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/games_fps.html). The example's level and external models were not imported. Original upstream MIT notices remain with the vendored code. `src/fps/physics.mjs` adapts `Octree.split` in a bounded `FloorOctree` subclass to propagate subdivision limits to children; intersection algorithms remain upstream.

## Yuka 0.7.8 — MIT

Copyright © 2022 Yuka authors. Full upstream license: [licenses/yuka-LICENSE](licenses/yuka-LICENSE).

Source: [Yuka](https://github.com/Mugen87/yuka), exact npm release `0.7.8`, locally vendored in `vendor/yuka/yuka.module.js`. AI executes Yuka `MemorySystem`/`MemoryRecord`, `StateMachine`, `Vehicle` and `SeekBehavior`; navigation uses `Graph`, `NavNode`, `NavEdge` and `AStar` with routes generated from the accepted floor and project-specific team/objective behavior.

[Dive](https://github.com/Mugen87/dive) was inspected for perception/steering organization; no Dive models, audio, map or navmesh was imported. Dive's code license does not license its mixed-source assets. No Valve or Counter-Strike assets are used.

## Kenney Furniture Kit — CC0 1.0

Source: [official Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit). The downloaded archive's license identifies **Furniture Kit 2.0**, while the website update list says 1.0; the archive SHA-256, not a floating version label, identifies these bytes. License copied unchanged to [licenses/kenney-furniture-kit-CC0.txt](licenses/kenney-furniture-kit-CC0.txt).

Selected self-contained GLBs: `chairDesk`, `computerScreen`, `bookcaseClosedDoors`, and `pottedPlant` in `assets/fps/models/`. Source geometry/material bytes are unchanged; runtime normalizes origins and scales models to the shared physical furniture envelopes. These are standard furnishing assumptions, not evidence of exact real-world inventory. All four selected package previews were visually inspected; bounding coordinates were inspected for every selected GLB.

## Kenney Impact Sounds 1.0 and Sci-Fi Sounds 1.0 — CC0 1.0

Sources: [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), [Sci-Fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds). Licenses copied unchanged to [Impact license](licenses/kenney-impact-sounds-CC0.txt) and [Sci-Fi license](licenses/kenney-sci-fi-sounds-CC0.txt).

Fifteen selected `.ogg` files in `assets/fps/audio/` are decoded and actually played by `src/fps/audio.mjs`: three carpet footsteps, three concrete footsteps, metal/soft/generic impacts, two explosion layers, a laser transient and a computer cue. Concrete footsteps are pitch-adjusted as a stylized vinyl proxy; the pack does not claim a vinyl recording. Runtime filtering, pitch, distance, wall damping and volume adapt the source samples. Additional original Web Audio synthesis supplies per-weapon transients, plastic-bag rustle, grenade hiss, bomb beeps and result cues. No audio source bytes are altered.

All three Kenney archives explicitly permit personal, educational and commercial use under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Attribution is optional; credit is retained here: **Kenney — www.kenney.nl**.

Playwright `1.55.1` (`@playwright/test`, Apache-2.0) runs browser acceptance checks. It is not a runtime network dependency and is not shipped as game code. Its package contains the upstream license in `node_modules/@playwright/test/LICENSE` after `npm ci`.

## ambientCG and Poly Haven — four local PBR sets, CC0 1.0

Created using **Fabric045**, **Concrete034** and **Wood051** from ambientCG.com, by Lennart Demes, licensed under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal License. [Provider license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/) explicitly covers redistribution of raw assets in games. Full legal code retained in `licenses/ambientcg-CC0-1.0.txt`.

Official sources: [Fabric045](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric045), [Concrete034](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete034), [Wood051](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Wood051). Their dated **1K-JPG** downloads are pinned by archive and individual file SHA-256 in `docs/fps-assets.json`. Only twelve required color/OpenGL-normal/roughness JPEGs are shipped in `assets/fps/materials/`; no high resolution archive, displacement, blend or USD assets are included.

`src/fps/materials.mjs` actually loads these maps locally. Colors use sRGB, normal/roughness use data space, UVs are in metres. Source bytes are unchanged. Runtime tinting gives carpet its blue office palette and walls warm off-white paint. Concrete034 is a smooth concrete source adapted with low relief and a subdued green tint as an **artistic epoxy/vinyl proxy**, not a claim that the provider recorded a vinyl material. Wood keeps its source grain. The fourth set is [White Plaster 02 by Rob Tuytel, Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_plaster_02), downloaded as three 1K JPEG maps. [Poly Haven licenses its assets CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license); the full legal code is retained in `licenses/polyhaven-CC0-1.0.txt`. Individual upstream URLs/MD5 and local SHA-256 values pin these files. The color-map contribution is reduced at runtime to 14% for walls and 10% for ceilings to represent opaque paint over bare plaster; standard PBR normal/roughness remain. Filename normalization changes no source bytes. Each selected color image was visually inspected. Private floor photos were not uploaded to either provider.

The synthetic screen/paper atlases, geometric cups, folders, jackets, cables, cartridge cartons, whiteboard diagrams and restrained sole scuffs in `src/fps/decor.mjs` are original project content. They contain no actual workplace data. New ambient ventilation/equipment hum and damage cues in `src/fps/audio.mjs` use original Web Audio synthesis plus the already licensed Kenney impacts. Audio perception remains separate from bot hearing.

## Original action presentation (0.8)

`action-models.mjs` and the bearded fantasy dwarf geometry in `action-visuals.mjs` are original project geometry. They do not use the uploaded planner's Kenney character assets or commercial game models. Existing Quaternius humanoid licensing remains unchanged. Seven `action-*.ogg` files are local synthetic utterances, generated by `tools/action/generate_audio.py` using eSpeak and processed by FFmpeg. Neither executable is bundled. See `licenses/action-audio.txt` and per-file hashes in `docs/fps-assets.json`. No commercial game voice recordings were copied.

## Facility sequence additions (private review, 2026-10-25)

Six recorded reload-mechanism sounds come from **The Free Firearm Sound Library**, Ben Jaszczak et al. / Still North Media, CC0-1.0. The unchanged processed MP3 cuts were retrieved from Riposte: Frontline commit `31ca050d8ce59f4885c01a3237f68cdf186c9b60`; its NOTICE preserves the source licenses for these sounds. Per-cue source recordings, edits, file hashes and credits are in `docs/fps-assets.json`; full CC0 terms are in `licenses/free-firearm-sound-library-CC0.txt`. That original six-cue integration did not include Riposte engine code; the additional licensed integration below is recorded separately.

Six **NORD-01** model types (nine selected GLBs) are original project-owned procedural assets. The runtime derivatives restore the source's bevel modifiers; repeated fixtures share GPU resources. See `licenses/nord01-original.txt`. Their explicit public release approval remains pending. The full portfolio, editable master and private source references are excluded from runtime packaging.

## Riposte integration (2026-10-26)

Selected MIT code from BenDBSS / Riposte: Frontline 5.6.0, commit `31ca050d8ce59f4885c01a3237f68cdf186c9b60`, is adapted via the imported Reuse Lab at `e2c94bf847c905fd3d9021f7e17d26a3ff72a298`: panel-door construction, pallet prefab, bounded instanced decal pool, local PBR loader, wrapped-angle steering and animation easing. The runtime uses nine CC0 paintwood/steel/timber maps and 25 CC0 weapon, impact and foley cuts (479,035 bytes combined). Original bytes, per-file sources and hashes are recorded in `docs/fps-assets.json` and `reviews/responsiveness-riposte/reuse-provenance.json`.

`licenses/riposte-MIT.txt`, `licenses/riposte-NOTICE.md` and `licenses/riposte-sfx-credits.json` ship with the filtered runtime. No restricted animation packs, commercial voices, music, trademarks, map geometry or external services are imported. Existing Cartridge and floor geometry retain their separate provenance.
